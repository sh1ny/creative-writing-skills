Use the cw-story-critique skill to analyze and give feedback.

Before critiquing, ask:
1. What scope? (beat / scene / chapter / act / full outline)
2. What kind of feedback? (big picture, line-level, structural, harsh)
3. What's the draft stage?

Adapt the critique to scope:
- Beat: dramatization, sensory grounding, POV consistency
- Scene: goal/conflict/outcome/emotional shift present?
- Chapter: pacing, scene sequence, chapter arc
- Outline: structural integrity, template alignment

After critique, suggest next action: /bs to brainstorm fixes, /outline to revise structure, /codex to check character consistency.

> **Agent tip:** Use the `cw-plan` primary agent.
