Use the cw-outline skill to develop the story outline top-down.

Remember the levels:
1. Act structure (Story/Outline/act-structure.md) — must be approved before moving down
2. Chapter outlines (Story/Outline/Act1/chapter-01.md) — one act at a time
3. Scene breakdowns with beat lists — for specific chapters

Always read existing outline files and Meta/project.md before generating.
Never skip levels — get approval at each stage before drilling down.
Beats in scene outlines are SHORT (1 sentence) — they're instructions for /beat.

> **Agent tip:** Use the `cw-plan` primary agent for structure discipline and critique.
